# Storyteller: SHAKESPEARE

Spoil the ending, then let the viewer watch a proud hero walk into it. Laugh at peak tension. The lowest-status person gives the tip. The hero says «كنت أنا». A rhymed couplet seals it over a family gathering.

Optional depth, not needed at runtime: `research/5-shakespeare.md` (style bible, sources, early tests, blind exam 20/20). How the set was built: `METHOD.md`.

**Brand layer.** Cast voices, logo, visual taste (for example a required transformation moment), which video types may talk, and banned copy (contact details, prices, offer wording, themes) come from your house rules: `skills/storyteller/house-rules.example.md`, or your own file in its place.

**Language.** Write every spoken line in the language or dialect the brief names, then run the matching voice module (see `.claude/skills/storyteller/VOICE-MODULES.md`). The craft rules work in any language. The examples, signature lines and dialect notes in this file are Arabic, written for the example module `storyteller-voice-palestinian`. For another language, keep the pattern (formula, epithet, retort shape, rhyme) and write fresh lines in that language.

## Arabic example: Palestinian dialect notes and voice (when the brief asks for it)

- Every spoken line is in **Palestinian Levantine** dialect, easy for Saudi viewers: شو، هيك، هلّق، بدّي/بدّو، كتير، منيح، مش، رح، ليش، وين، هاد/هاي، ابعت، إيد. No rare village slang, no Egyptian, no fusha speeches, no «يا مولاي».
- House spelling **«الي»**, never «اللي».
- Avoid Gulf markers: وش، يبي/أبغى، الحين، مرّه (= very), زين as "ok". The early test scripts in the bible use another dialect: reuse their structure, never their wording.
- Typical cast: الراوي (narrator) · an older man (here أبو سالم) · a young woman (هالة) · an older woman (سعاد). Voice choice per role comes from your house rules; extra roles only if the brief names a new voice.
- Rhymes must rhyme when spoken Palestinian (say them aloud: محال/الرجال, جديد/بيزيد, قصدير/بيستشير). Near-rhymes only in the couplet, never in prose lines.

## When to pick / when NOT

**Pick** when the brief has: a family or friends cast · comedy · a hero proud of one thing · a mistake the viewer can see coming · a learning moment for someone stubborn (elder, boaster, expert) · a known ritual, season or news event to retell · a concrete tip that a "low" character can deliver.

**Do NOT pick** when:
- Silent video types: if your house rules forbid talking in this video type (e.g. a silent fashion or catalogue film), this master needs speech, so pass.
- 15 s product shot with no cast conflict (Aesop is sharper).
- A series hook / part-2 cliffhanger is the goal (Scheherazade).
- Celebration or pride with no fall (Homer).
- Quiet business wisdom with no comedy (Ibn al-Muqaffa').
- The theme is fear or danger. Comedy needs a safe fall.

## Rules (bible numbering)

- **R1 Tell the ending first.** Line 1 (narrator) gives away the outcome. Tension moves from whether to how. Plain dialect, not a fusha prophecy.
- **R2 Cold open mid-pulse.** First image already charged: raised object, smoke, a buzz, a boast. No greeting, no setup.
- **R3 One hero, one trait, trait causes trouble.** Pride, haste, stubbornness, overconfidence. Never pure bad luck.
- **R4 Freeze and confess.** World freezes mid-gesture; hero tells the lens the truth he hides from others. Audience now knows more than the cast.
- **R5 Irony gap.** At least one beat where viewers know what a character does not (spoiled end, hidden sender, secret plan).
- **R6 Two plots, one mirror.** A low funny subplot repeats the main problem in another key (burning rice / burning oud; spilled coffee / shaky phone hand). It carries the answer.
- **R7 The fool tells the truth.** Tip comes from the lowest-status, funniest person (kid, cook, granddaughter), as a joke or jab.
- **R8 Shift register.** Hero and narrator: rhymed, balanced half-lines. Kids, cooks, the fool: fast loose prose, may overlap. Hero drops to plain prose at the break.
- **R9 One pun carries the theme.** A word that turns (محال، ثوب، عود، مبارك/مبروك).
- **R10 Turn in the middle.** At 45–55% of runtime one event flips direction.
- **R11 Reversal + recognition together.** Fortune flips and the hero names his trait in plain words: «كنت أنا».
- **R12 Comic relief at peak tension.** A laugh directly before or after the heaviest beat.
- **R13 Disguise / mistaken identity.** Something or someone taken for what it is not; unmasking = payoff.
- **R14 Borrow the known, move the spotlight.** Familiar ritual, news or shop problem; drama goes to a minor figure inside it.
- **R15 Rhymed couplet over a gathering.** Two rhymed half-lines carry the tip; comedy ends in a feast or family circle.

**Anti-rules (any one = rewrite)**
1. Flawless hero beaten by luck or a villain only.
2. One flat register: all prose, or all rhyme.
3. Hidden ending saved for a twist.
4. Narrator or any character states a general moral the hero never discovers.
5. Unbroken solemnity, or unbroken jokes.

**Fake imitations (never)**
1. Costume Shakespeare: fusha «يا مولاي», ruffs, skull, "to be or not to be".
2. Death as the Shakespeare marker.
3. Explaining monologue: an aside that recaps plot instead of confessing a secret.
4. Rhyme wallpaper: every character rhymes every line.
5. Twist ending.

**Fable-drift guard** (judge's note on an early football test): no metaphor demo that turns the tip into a parable (cups-as-team); no character speaking a general maxim («لا تخاف من الاسم»); no «العبرة»; the tip is concrete steps; meaning lives in «كنت أنا» + the couplet only.

## Time maps

**15 s:** 0–2 spoiler over charged image (R1 R2) · 2–5 rhymed boast (R3 R8) · 5–8 freeze-confess (R4 R5) · 8–12 fool's one-line tip, laugh, cutaway subplot in one shot (R6 R7 R12) · 12–15 «كنت أنا» folded into couplet over the group (R11 R15). Turn = the fool's line at ~7–8 s.

**30 s:** 0–3 spoiler · 3–7 boast · 7–11 freeze-confess · 11–15 subplot + fool's jab, laugh · 15–17 turn (pun lands) · 17–23 tip steps on screen (transformation moment here, if used) · 23–26 «كنت أنا», plain prose · 26–30 couplet, wide on the gathering.

**45 s (default):**
| Time | Beat | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 | Narrator spoils ending over one charged detail | R1 R2 |
| 3–9 | Hero's rhymed boast, trait on show, low angle | R3 R8 |
| 9–15 | Problem bites; freeze; hero confesses to lens, half-voice, push-in | R4 R5 |
| 15–22 | Mirror subplot; fool in fast prose; laugh | R6 R7 R12 |
| 20–25 | Turn: fool's jab holds the answer; pun close-up | R9 R10 |
| 25–33 | Tip shown step by step (hands/phone close-ups; transformation moment here, if used) | value |
| 33–39 | Reversal + «كنت أنا», hero to lens, softer light | R11 R13 |
| 39–45 | Gathering; rhymed couplet with the tip; tip card | R15 |

**60 s:** 45 s map plus: the subplot fails first, then succeeds (second mirror beat, 15–28 s), and a second short aside before the turn (~26 s). Turn at 28–32 s. Still one hero, one tip, one couplet.

Keep at every length: spoiler, one freeze-confess, fool's tip, mid turn, «كنت أنا», couplet. Drop always: deaths, real tragedy, long verse, more than one subplot.

## Signature lines bank (Arabic examples, Palestinian; adapt the pattern)

Ending-first openers
1. «الليلة عرس سامي… وهو بإيده رح يخرّبه، والطبّاخ بكلمة رح يصلّحه.»
2. «أبو سالم رح يخسر الرهان قبل العشا… والكل رح يضحك إلا هو.»
3. «هاي الكيكة رح تنباع كلها بكرا… بس مش قبل ما سعاد تعترف بغلطتها.»
4. «الليلة هالة رح تنحرج قدّام العيلة كلها… وتطلع هي الأشطر بالقعدة.»

Freeze asides (everyone stops; hero to lens)
5. «بيني وبينكم؟ عمري ما سألت حدا… ومش رح أبلّش هلّق.»
6. «قدّامهم واثق… بس الريحة محروقة، وأنا دافع فيها راتب شهر.»
7. «خلّيهم يفكّروها هالة… أنا ساكت، وانتو شهود.»

«كنت أنا» recognitions (plain prose, no rhyme)
8. «ما كان العود الغلطان… كنت أنا، الي ما بيسأل.»
9. «الكيكة ما تغيّرت… كنت أنا الي فكّرت الطعم بيكفّي.»
10. «مش الجهاز الصعب… كنت أنا العنيد.»

Rhymed couplet closers (carry the tip, not a sermon)
11. «خلّي الجمرة تشيب، وغطّيها بقصدير… بيطيب العود، وبيطيب الي بيستشير.»
12. «ضو الشبّاك وصحن أبيض، والكيكة لحالها… الصورة الحلوة بتبيع، قبل ما حدا يذوق حلاها.»
13. «اضغط مطوّل عالصورة، بيطلع ملصق جديد… والي بيتعلّم بالسبعين، ما بينقص، بيزيد.»

Patterns only; never paste a bank line into a different story.

## Lendable move (MIX MODE, off by default)

**The ONE move to lend: the freeze-and-confess aside (R4).**
How a host master inserts it without breaking its own style:
- One beat only, 3–5 s, placed before the host's turn or ending.
- Everyone else freezes mid-gesture (steam/smoke hangs); key light narrows; push-in.
- The host's lead character speaks ONE hidden truth to the lens in plain prose. No rhyme, no moral, no recap, no tip.
- Snap back: the world resumes on the next frame; the host's structure continues unchanged.
- Aesop: the boaster confesses before the fall. Scheherazade: a listener in the frame confesses, then the tale resumes. Ibn al-Muqaffa': the questioner's secret motive, outside the nested tale. Homer: the hero's private doubt between boasts.

**Never lend:** R1 spoiler opening (kills Scheherazade suspense, clashes with Homer invocation) · R15 couplet close (collides with Aesop moral, Kalila «وهذا مثل…», Nights cliffhanger) · R6/R7 fool subplot (needs runtime; becomes a second story) · R8 register shift (needs the whole piece).

**When Shakespeare hosts:** may accept one Homer epithet on the boaster (feeds R3). Never accept talking animals, a nested tale, or a cliffhanger cut (breaks R15).

## Writing steps

1. **Brief.** Use the conductor's STORY BRIEF. Standalone: write a 9-line mini-brief first (goal · audience · platform · length · shape 9:16/16:9 · product · ONE takeaway · feeling · single/series · talking allowed yes/no · house rules). If talking is not allowed, stop and hand back: wrong master.
2. **Beats.** Fill the time map for the length: hero + trait, the fool, the mirror subplot, the pun, the disguise, the gathering, the concrete tip (steps a viewer can copy today).
3. **Script.** Numbered lines, one speaker each: `1. (0:00–0:03) الراوي: «…»`. Stage notes in brackets. Roles only from your cast. Rhyme only for hero boast, narrator, couplet.
4. **Tip card.** Text = the spoken tip, word for word, from the fool's line. Short, inside the platform safe zone (e.g. x 86–994, y 230–1536 of 1080x1920).
5. **Shot list.** One row per line: framing, action, freeze moments, the one surprise, and the transformation moment if your house rules ask for one.
6. **Self-check.** Fill the proof list below. Any miss = fix, then hand over (the conductor sends it to the voice module next; example: `storyteller-voice-palestinian`).

## Self-check proof list

- R1–R15 each with the line number(s) that hit it; R13 and R14 may be partial, all others required.
- Turn line timestamp sits at 45–55% of runtime.
- Anti-rules 1–5 absent; fake imitations 1–5 absent; fable-drift guard passed (no maxim line, no parable demo).
- ONE takeaway; one-sentence walk-away value; tip shown as real steps on screen.
- Tip card = spoken tip, word for word.
- Voice: the brief's language throughout, through its voice module (Palestinian example: «الي» only; no Gulf markers); the couplet rhymes aloud in that language.
- Talking rule: silent video types per house rules never use this master.
- No real person's face, voice or invented words (news figures shown from behind, never speak).
- House-rules checks passed (themes, banned copy, brand marks, visual taste).
- One surprise present.

## Model script: «ملصق أبو سالم» (45 s, 9:16)

Brief: teach a phone trick · Saudi families on Reels/TikTok · ONE takeaway: long-press a person in a photo, choose "sticker", send · feeling: warm laugh · single post.

1. (0:00–0:04) الراوي: «أبو سالم، سبعين سنة… الليلة رح يضحّك القروب كلّه، وما حدا رح يعرف إنّه هو.»
2. (0:04–0:09) أبو سالم (بيبعد الجوال بعكّازته): «أنا الي ربّيت الحلال وعلّمت الرجال… وهالجهاز بدّو يغلبني؟ هاد والله محال.»
3. (0:09–0:12) (هالة بتصبّ القهوة وبتكبّ عالصينية) أبو سالم: «الدلّة بدها إيد ثابتة يا بنتي… زيّ الكلمة.»
4. (0:12–0:14) هالة (بتضحك): «علّمني الدلّة، وبعلّمك الجوال. اتّفقنا؟»
5. (0:14–0:22) هالة (ماسكة إيد جدّها عالشاشة): «اضغط مطوّل عالشخص بالصورة… شوف، انفصل لحاله. اختار "ملصق"، وابعته.»
6. (0:22–0:27) (الكل بيتجمّد، أبو سالم للكاميرا): «تعلّمت بدقيقة الي حسبوه عليّ محال… وهلّق؟ خلّيهم يفكّروها هالة.»
7. (0:27–0:32) (أبو سالم بيبعت للقروب ملصق وجه هالة والقهوة مكبوبة) سعاد (بتضحك عجوالها): «هالة! اتركي جوال جدّك، شو هالملصق؟… وابعتيلي واحد!»
8. (0:32–0:34) هالة (جوالها بإيدها، مطفي): «والله ما لمست جواله!»
9. (0:34–0:40) أبو سالم (بيعمل ملصق تاني من وجهه هو، وبيبعته برسالة صوتية): «أنا الي قلت محال… مش الجهاز الصعب، كنت أنا العنيد. وبكرا بعلّمكم.»
10. (0:40–0:45) الراوي: «اضغط مطوّل عالصورة، بيطلع ملصق جديد… والي بيتعلّم بالسبعين، ما بينقص، بيزيد.»
