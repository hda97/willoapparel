# Storyteller: Scheherazade

A teller on screen bargains with a listener who holds power. The teller opens a small tale that pays a real tip. An outside clock cuts the teller off at the doorway of a stranger tale.

**Depth (optional, not needed at runtime):** `research/2-scheherazade.md` (bible: sources, close reading, scored tests). The early test shot lists are not included; they used an older dialect, so their structure was kept, never their wording. How the set was built: `METHOD.md`.

**Brand layer.** Cast voices, logo, visual taste (for example a required transformation moment), which video types may talk, and banned copy (contact details, prices, offer wording, themes) come from your house rules: `skills/storyteller/house-rules.example.md`, or your own file in its place.

**Language.** Write every spoken line in the language or dialect the brief names, then run the matching voice module (see `.claude/skills/storyteller/VOICE-MODULES.md`). The craft rules work in any language. The examples, signature lines and dialect notes in this file are Arabic, written for the example module `storyteller-voice-palestinian`. For another language, keep the pattern (formula, epithet, retort shape, rhyme) and write fresh lines in that language.

## 0. Input

- **From the conductor:** use the STORY BRIEF as given. It covers goal, audience, platform, length, shape, product, ONE takeaway, feeling, single/series, talking allowed or not, and house rules.
- **Standalone:** first write a 9-line mini-brief with the same fields. Defaults: 45 s, 9:16, single post, talking video. Ask at most 1 question, and only if the takeaway is missing.

## 1. When to pick / when not

**Pick it** for any of these:
- a series, or viewers who must come back;
- curiosity as the feeling;
- a tip that works as "an old tale that fixes today's problem";
- a natural power listener: father, client, coach, grandmother;
- a natural outside clock: zaffa, oven, adhan, "sleep!".

**Do not pick it** for:
- **Silent video types.** If your house rules forbid talking in this video type (e.g. a silent fashion or model film), send the brief back: this style lives on spoken bargaining. If forced, the silent footage is a proof shot only.
- A lesson under 15 s (Aesop), pride (Homer), a mistake we watch coming (Shakespeare), calm business wisdom (Ibn al-Muqaffa').
- Any fear, jinn-threat or death stake. The stake is always social.

## 2. Rules (bible numbering)

- **R1 Stake now.** Line 1 puts something at risk within minutes or hours (zaffa in 15 min, order tonight, final tomorrow). The stake is social, never death.
- **R2 Power listener.** An on-screen character grants or refuses. The teller works on that character, not on the camera.
- **R3 Ransom out loud.** «إذا حكيتلك حكاية أعجب من…، بتعطيني…؟» The price is small and specific.
- **R4 Doorway.** Open the inner tale with «بلغني يا … السعيد، إنّه…», fitted to the listener. This is the only fusha in the video.
- **R5 Newcomers carry stories.** Every new face brings the fix or part of the tale. The clock voice is exempt.
- **R6 Nesting with a job.** The inner tale mirrors the outer problem and holds its fix. Use 2 levels at most.
- **R7 Wonder pays.** Say «عجيب / أعجب / أغرب». The listener judges the tale wondrous on screen, and that is the payment.
- **R8 Threes, third turned.** Two beats near word for word, then a third that breaks the pattern with the fix.
- **R9 Exact repetition.** The same phrase returns at each beat («جمرة حمرا… دخان أسود»).
- **R10 Trifle fate.** One small object causes the problem and holds the cure: coal, window light, search box, offside flag. Show the coincidence; never explain it.
- **R11 Wit beats size.** The weaker side wins with a clever move, not force or money. The tip IS that move.
- **R12 Tip shown working.** Speak the takeaway once in a plain line and prove it on screen. The tip card shows on that line.
- **R13 Pay before the cut.** The inner tale closes and the tip is paid. Only the NEXT tale is withheld.
- **R14 Outbid promise.** The last line names a stranger tale with one concrete object («وأعجب منها حكاية…») and stops mid-word.
- **R15 Clock cuts.** An outside sound ends it, then a hard cut or freeze. No outro card, never "follow for part 2".

**Anti-rules.** Any one of these fails the script:
- **X1:** a closing moral («والعبرة…»).
- **X2:** an animal cast, or a "king asks his sage" frame (that is Kalila).
- **X3:** inner monologue or backstory VO.
- **X4:** a calm ending, or the outer frame closing within one episode.
- **X5:** decorative nesting, or a third level.

**Fakes to reject:** genie/lamp/carpet décor · "once upon a time in Baghdad" with no frame · a bait cliffhanger that pays nothing · saj' and archaic fusha all through · harem exoticism.

## 3. Arabic example: Palestinian dialect notes and voices (when the brief asks for it)

- **Dialect:** every spoken line is Palestinian Levantine. Spell it «الي», never «اللي». Keep it easy for Saudi viewers, with no rare slang.
- **Use:** شو، هلّق، بدّي، مش، كتير، منيح، هيك، إشي، عطيني، حكيتلك، لحدّ ما، يابا، خلص، التانية/التالتة.
- **Swap out these Gulf markers:** وش، الحين، مو، أبي، زين، يبه، وليدي، الديرة، ملكة (write «كتب الكتاب»).
- **Sentences:** keep each one to a breath, 12 words or fewer. Speak numbers the dialect way.
- **Voices:** the teller is seen on screen (no off-screen VO). Typical roles: an older man as the power listener, a young woman, an older woman or grandmother. Voice choice per role comes from your house rules.
- **Length:** about 2–2.5 words per second (45 s ≈ 85–105 words). The voice module for the brief's language polishes afterwards (example: `storyteller-voice-palestinian`).

## 4. Time maps

- **15 s** (R1–R3, R7, R12, R14 short, R15; no nesting):
  - 0–3 stake
  - 3–6 bargain
  - 6–11 one-line tale with the fix, then «عجيب»
  - 11–13 tip line and card, shown working
  - 13–15 outbid, then the clock cuts
- **30 s** (adds R4, R8, R9):
  - 0–3 stake
  - 3–7 bargain and accept
  - 7–10 «بلغني» and a visual transition into the tale
  - 10–17 beats 1–2, same words
  - 17–21 beat 3 turned, with the fix
  - 21–26 «عجيب», tip line, card and grant
  - 26–30 outbid and clock
- **45 s** (all 15 rules):
  - 0–3 stake
  - 3–8 bargain
  - 8–9 accept
  - 9–15 doorway and the transformation moment: smoke, a lid or a ball opens into a miniature world
  - 15–20 beats 1–2, same camera move
  - 20–26 a newcomer speaks the fix, lip-synced
  - 26–30 beat 3 turned
  - 30–40 real room: the trifle changes, «عجيب», tip line and card, grant
  - 40–45 outbid, then a hard cut on the clock
- **60 s** (2 levels at most):
  - 0–3 stake
  - 3–9 bargain and accept
  - 9–15 doorway
  - 15–24 beats 1–2
  - 24–33 newcomer with the fix
  - 33–38 beat 3
  - 38–48 proof, «عجيب», tip and grant
  - 48–55 a planted requester («كمّل! وبعدين شو صار؟»), then the outbid
  - 55–60 the clock cuts

**SERIES map ("nights")**
1. **Each post stands alone.** Its inner tale closes and its tip is paid. The cut falls on the next tale's doorway, never inside a tip.
2. **The promise becomes the next post.** Post N's R14 object becomes post N+1's inner tale. N+1 opens with the same teller and a 2 s recap («بلغني يا … إنّ الشماغ الي…»), and pays the promise within 10 s.
3. **Same frame, rising stake.** Keep the teller and the listener. Raise the stake each night: zaffa, then dinner, then the first morning. The tale, object and tip change every post.
4. **Viewers ask for more.** Pin «كمّل؟». The best reply can be voiced at the start of the next post.
5. **Mark the night.** Add a «الليلة ٢» caption in the safe zone, and put the promised object on the cover. Value is never held hostage.

## 5. Signature lines (Arabic examples, Palestinian; adapt the pattern)

1. **Wager:** «إذا حكيتلك حكاية أعجب من هيك، بتعطيني …؟»
2. **Soft wager:** «عطيني دقيقتين بس… وإذا ما عجبتك، بسكت.»
3. **Accept:** «احكي… والدقايق عليك.»
4. **Doorway:** «بلغني يا أبو العريس السعيد، إنّه…»
5. **Doorway variants:** «بلغني يا كابتن السعيد، إنّه…» / «بلغني يا معلّمتي السعيدة، إنّه…» (match the gender)
6. **Triad:** «حطّ الأولى… دخان أسود. حطّ التانية… دخان أسود.»
7. **Newcomer:** «ودخلت عليه جدّته، وقالتله:»
8. **Newcomer invited:** «وإنتِ، شو حكايتك؟»
9. **Verdict:** «عجيب! نفس الإشي؟»
10. **Verdict and grant:** «والله إشي عجيب… خلص، الطلب إلك.»
11. **Outbid:** «وأعجب منها حكاية … الي …، يوم…»
12. **Requester:** «كمّل! وبعدين شو صار؟»
13. **Clock cuts:** «يابا، الزفّة!» · «الفرن!» · «يلّا عالنوم، بكرا عندك نهائي!» · or the adhan starts and the teller smiles.
14. **Inner-tale saying** (never a closing moral): «العود ما بينحرق، العود بيتنفّس.»

## 6. Lendable move (MIX MODE)

**Lend: the paid outbid with a clock cut**, R13 + R14 + R15 as one ending unit.

Insert it like this:
- Replace only the host's last 2–4 s.
- The host's takeaway must already be spoken and shown before it.
- Plant the clock earlier in the host scene: drums heard, an oven in view, a mother next door.
- Write one line in the host speaker's voice: «وأعجب منها حكاية [object] الي…، يوم…». Then the sound cuts it.
- Add no «بلغني», bargain or «عجيب» unless the host already has them.
- With Aesop, his ≤8-word moral lands first, then the cut.

**Never lend these.** Each one rebuilds the structure, so the host stops being itself:
- the bargain with a power listener (R1–R3);
- the «بلغني» doorway and nesting (R4–R6);
- wonder as payment (R7);
- the cliffhanger without R13. That is the bait fake.

## 7. Writing steps

1. **Brief.** Use the conductor's brief, or write the mini-brief (§0). Write the ONE takeaway as one sentence a viewer can act on today.
2. **Beats.** Fill 10 slots before any dialogue:
   - stake and clock;
   - power listener;
   - teller;
   - ransom;
   - trifle object;
   - inner-tale mirror;
   - newcomer and fix;
   - triad phrase;
   - tip line (10 words or fewer);
   - next-tale object.
   Include one surprise, and the transformation moment if your house rules ask for one.
3. **Script.** Number and time every line. Give one speaker per line, with a role tag: `1. (0:00–0:03) أبو سالم [older man]: «…»`. Put actions in brackets.
4. **Tip card.** Use the spoken tip line, word for word. Keep it under 10 words, in the lower third inside the platform safe zone (e.g. x 86–994 / y 230–1536 of 1080x1920), clear of faces, on screen about 3 s.
5. **Shot sketch.** Write one line per block: camera, transition into the tale, card, cut frame. `storyteller-shotlist` expands it.
6. **Self-check.** Run §8 and fix every miss. Output: brief → script → card → shots → proof.

## 8. Self-check proof list (print it)

- **Rules:** list each of R1–R15 with its line numbers. At 15/30 s, mark the dropped rules "n/a by map".
- **Absent:** X1–X5 and all 5 fakes.
- **One takeaway:** quote it. It is paid before the cut line and shown working.
- **Tip card:** it matches the spoken tip line word for word. Quote both.
- **Talking:** the cast talks, lip-synced. Silent video types per house rules do not use this master.
- **Real people:** no real face, look-alike, voice or invented words. Real events use faceless token figures and checked facts.
- **Style bans:** no fear, jinn-threat or death stake; no "part 2" bait.
- **House rules:** themes, banned copy, brand marks and visual taste checked against your house-rules file.
- **Voice:** every line in the brief's language, through its voice module (Palestinian example: «الي», no Gulf markers). The word count fits the length.
- **Series:** the next object is named, the «الليلة N» caption is set and the pinned comment is ready.

## 9. Model script: "The groom's oud" (45 s, 9:16)

**Takeaway:** set oud on coal only once the coal greys under white ash. Red coal burns the wood.

1. (0:00–0:03) أبو سالم [older man] (يلوّح بالدخان): «العود ريحته محروقة! والزفّة بعد ربع ساعة… ارموه.»
2. (0:03–0:08) العطّار [teller]: «لا، لا ترميه. عطيني دقيقتين: إذا حكيتلك حكاية أعجب من هالدخان، العود بيدخل الزفّة.»
3. (0:08–0:09) أبو سالم: «احكي… والدقايق عليك.»
4. (0:09–0:15) العطّار: «بلغني يا أبو العريس السعيد، إنّه عريس بالبلد قبل خمسين سنة، حطّ أوّل قطعة على جمرة حمرا… دخان أسود.»
5. (0:15–0:18) العطّار: «حطّ التانية على جمرة حمرا… دخان أسود.»
6. (0:18–0:20) العطّار: «ودخلت عليه جدّته، وقالتله:»
7. (0:20–0:26) الجدّة [older woman]: «يا حبيبي، العود ما بينحرق، العود بيتنفّس. خلّي الجمرة تشيب، يغطّيها رماد أبيض، وبعدين حطّه.»
8. (0:26–0:30) العطّار: «حطّ التالتة على جمرة شايبة… وريحتها وصلت آخر الحارة.»
9. (0:30–0:34) (سالم بينفخ الجمرة لحدّ ما تشيب وبيحطّ القطعة… الريحة بتفوح) أبو سالم: «عجيب! نفس العود؟»
10. (0:34–0:38) العطّار: «نفس العود، بس النار تغيّرت. حطّ العود على جمرة شايبة، مش حمرا.»
11. (0:38–0:40) أبو سالم: «دخّلوه الزفّة.»
12. (0:40–0:44) العطّار: «وأعجب منها حكاية الشماغ الي انكوى ليلة كتب كتاب أبوك، يوم…»
13. (0:44–0:45) (طبول الزفّة بتضرب) هالة [young woman]: «يابا، الزفّة!» (العطّار بيسكت وبيبتسم)

**Tip card (0:34–0:38):** «حطّ العود على جمرة شايبة، مش حمرا»

**Shots**
- **0:00–0:09** Night-and-gold majlis: black smoke from a gold mabkhara; Salem, the groom in a bisht, stands behind his father. The perfume seller holds up two fingers.
- **0:09–0:20** Transformation moment: the smoke builds a miniature old courtyard with an olive tree. A young groom sets oud on red coal twice, and the same black puff rises from the same angle.
- **0:20–0:26** The grandmother speaks L7. The red coal in her palm greys under ash.
- **0:26–0:30** Surprise: a pale scent ribbon flows out of the miniature into the real majlis.
- **0:30–0:40** Salem greys the coal and sets the chip. Clean smoke rises and the father's eyebrows go up. The card sits in the lower third, clear of faces.
- **0:40–0:45** L12. The drums hit and the doors flood with light. The teller stops mid-word and smiles. Hard cut on the first beat, with no outro.

- **Takeaway:** paid at L10, before the cut at L13. The card matches L10.
