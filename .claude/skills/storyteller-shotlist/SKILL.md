# Shot list (production handoff)

Input: the PASSED script (numbered lines with times), the brief (language, shape, length, cast), the tip card and the house rules.

## Output table (one row per shot)
| # | time | shot (camera, framing, motion) | on screen (cast, product, the transformation moment) | spoken line (voice) | on-screen text | sound |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Rules:
1. **Shape:** ONE shape only, 9:16 1080x1920 or 16:9 as the brief says.
2. **Voices:** take the voice per role from the house rules, plus the TTS style prompt of the voice module. Mark which lines are lip-synced on screen and which are voice-over off-screen.
3. **Silent video types:** if the house rules make a video type silent (for example model / fashion videos), use music + on-screen text only.
4. **Text:**
   - All text inside the platform safe zone (our 9:16 zone: x 86–994, y 230–1536 of 1080x1920).
   - One text at a time, never overlapping.
   - Labels never over a face.
5. **Tip card:** its own shot, the same words as the spoken tip, in the house graphic style.
6. **Brand:** logo, end card and disclosure per the house rules and the brief.
7. **Visual moments:** name the shot where the prop changes state, and the one surprise. If the house rules ask for a specific kind of moment (for example a 3D assembly), name it.
8. **Motion:** real motion in every shot (no still being zoomed). No fake transitions; cuts are motivated.
9. **Product:** the product leads at least one hero shot. The setting never hides the product (lesson from one of our oud covers, where the wedding hall upstaged the oud).
10. **Sound:** music mood, and where it ducks under the voice. Sound effects only where the house rules allow them.
11. **Cover:** propose `<id>-cover.jpg`: the frame + the cover text (safe zone).

End with: total length · word count per voice · list of assets needed · who makes it. Name no paid tool without its price and the requester's OK.
