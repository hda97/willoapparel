# House rules (example brand layer)

The master skills hold craft that works for anyone. Everything that belongs to one brand lives here, in one file. Every step of the chain reads it: the analyzer lists the rules that touch the topic, the master writes inside them, the reviewer checks them, the shot list applies them.

This is a trimmed, anonymised version of the rule layer we use at our own studio (an AI production studio selling product films and fashion/catalogue video to small shops). Copy it to `house-rules.md`, then replace every line with your own.

## 1. Language and audience
- Spoken language: Palestinian Levantine Arabic, run through `storyteller-voice-palestinian`. Audience: the Saudi market, so lines must be easy for Gulf viewers.
- House spelling «الي», never «اللي».
- Family codes for a Saudi audience: see law 11 in `skills/storyteller/SKILL.md`.

## 2. Cast and voices
- A small fixed cast keeps the feed recognisable: a narrator, an older man (the uncle / shop owner), a young woman (the niece / maker), an older woman (the aunt / neighbour). Fixed names per character help a series.
- TTS voices per role (we use Gemini TTS): narrator = Achird (calm), older man = Charon, young woman = Sulafat, older woman = Vindemiatrix. Style prompt for all: "Palestinian Levantine Arabic accent, warm, natural, conversational".
- Talking characters are lip-synced on screen.
- The studio owner never appears on camera and never voices anything.

## 3. Which videos talk
- Model / fashion / catalogue videos: NO talking. Music and on-screen text only, real-camera look, no AI transformation effects.
- Every other video: talking check, with vs without; keep the better one and say why.

## 4. Visual taste
- Every non-model post has a 3D cast on screen, one 3D assembly or transformation moment, and one surprise.
- Luxury look: night palette with gold and cyan accents; nothing cheap or plain.
- Logo in the top-left corner, fixed size and opacity, on every frame.
- No burned-in "made with AI" text; use the platform's AI-content toggle at upload.
- All text inside the safe zone x 86–994, y 230–1536 of 1080x1920; labels never over a face.

## 5. Banned themes
- No scam, fraud, fear or "safety warning" stories. (They did not perform for us and they do not fit the brand.)

## 6. Banned or fixed copy
- No phone or chat-app number in any video, caption or card. Contact is via the website and the brand's social profile only.
- Never write "see the result before you pay" or "see the work first". The offer is a free preview of ONE sample photo, named only in the fixed wording the brand approved.
- Prices: only from the live price list. Never invent a price, a discount or a package name in a script.
- Service names: only the official names from the price list.
- Every post gives usable value: one sentence the viewer can walk away with. (Our own ads are exempt from the tip, not from clarity.)

## 7. Process rules
- No script goes to the owner before the human reviewer passes it.
- One item at a time to the owner for approval.
- Paid generation (video, image, voice) only after stating model, cost and balance and getting a yes.

## 8. Names
- Never put a real person's name, face or voice in a script without their consent. Never name the owner or staff.
